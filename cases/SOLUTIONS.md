# The AI Files — case solutions (ORGANISERS ONLY)

Do not share this file or the `cases/` folder with participants. The same answers are visible to admins (Cases → open a case) and to judges after Close.

Each case is a short story with three suspects, 8 multiple-choice questions (one clue each) and, after the twist, 3 more questions. The story points at one suspect first; the clues show it was someone else. Careful teams can work out the real culprit before the twist; the twist confirms it for everyone.

Automatic score (50): round-1 questions 24 (3 each) · twist questions 12 (4 each) · Initial Conclusion names the real culprit 6 · Final Report names the real culprit 8. No negative marking. Judges add 50. Details: `docs/SCORING.md`.

## CASE-A · The Locked Room

**Who did it:** Sarah Menon (business partner)

**What happened.** It was murder staged as a burglary and a fall. Vikram had found that Sarah was moving company money and was ending the partnership. She came in at 10:12, had coffee with him (she takes sugar), they argued at about 10:45, and she struck him with the brass owl paperweight between his 10:36 message and 10:47, when the clock stopped. She wiped the paperweight, opened the window to fake a break-in, locked the door from outside with the spare key, dropped the key in the plant pot and left at 11:02. The dry sill, untouched flower bed, unmarked ladder and single head injury show the break-in and the fall were fake. Daniel's car was at the cinema until 11:01 and he reached the gate at 11:15. Meera was in the kitchen with the cook.

**Common wrong answer:** Daniel Rathore (nephew and heir)

**Round 1**

- **Q1** (The window) Which explanation fits every one of these facts? → **B** · The window was opened after the rain, and nobody climbed through it
- **Q2** (The ladder) What do the ladder and the doctor's note show together? → **C** · He was struck, and the ladder was placed to make it look like a fall
- **Q3** (Wall clock) What is the narrowest time window for the attack that all of this supports? → **D** · Between 10:36 PM and about 10:47 PM
- **Q4** (Paperweight) Only one object in the room was wiped. What does that tell us? → **A** · Someone took it from the desk, used it, and then tried to hide that
- **Q5** (Coffee cups) What can we safely conclude from the cups? → **C** · Vikram was expecting a visitor, and that visitor sat and drank with him
- **Q6** (Phone) Which statement from the case file does this message make most important to check? → **D** · Sarah's statement that she never entered the house
- **Q7** (Cinema) Could Daniel have attacked Vikram at about 10:47 PM? → **B** · No: his car stayed at the cinema until 11:01, 15 minutes away
- **Q8** (The door) If this was murder, what must be true? → **A** · There is a second key, and the killer knew where to find it

**Twist.** New evidence has come in: the gate guard's log, Vikram's diary and a second key to the study.

- **T1** (Gate log) Put the gate log beside the earlier clues. Which statement is true? → **A** · Sarah was on the property during the shouting and when the clock stopped
- **T2** (Spare key) Who could have locked the study door with the spare key that night? → **C** · Sarah: she knew about the key, and Meera was with the cook
- **T3** (Diary) What does the diary add to the 10:36 PM message? → **B** · It explains why he was ending the partnership, and why she could not let him reach his lawyer

**A good report** names the culprit, says how it was done, and points to at least two clues that prove it (and ideally one clue that clears the first suspect).

## CASE-B · The Leaked Question Paper

**Who did it:** Gopal Bhatia (department clerk)

**What happened.** Gopal Bhatia, the clerk, came back to Room 204 at 7:40 PM, opened the cupboard with the spare key from his drawer, photographed the paper and sent it to his son Aman, who has a backlog in the same subject. The photo reached Aman at 8:01 PM and he posted it at 8:05 PM. The red-ink correction proves the photo was taken after 6:30 PM, the cupboard was not forced and was locked again, and Bhatia's green mug is in the photo. His “I only came for my tiffin” story is false: he stayed 30 minutes, the tiffin was still on his desk next morning, and nobody else entered after 7:10. Rohan was in the library from 5:10 to 8:45, and Nisha was with the professor the whole time.

**Common wrong answer:** Rohan Verma (teaching assistant)

**Round 1**

- **Q1** (The leaked photo) What does the red-ink correction rule out? → **A** · That the photo was taken at any time before 6:30 PM
- **Q2** (Steel cupboard) What does this tell us about the leaker? → **B** · They used a key, and locked the paper away again afterwards
- **Q3** (Keys) After 6:45 PM, who could have opened the cupboard? → **D** · Anyone who got into Room 204 and knew about the drawer
- **Q4** (Library log) Rohan can enter Room 204. Why does that not make him the leaker? → **A** · The photo was taken after 6:30 PM, when he was in the library
- **Q5** (Professor's statement) Nisha was in Room 204 that evening. What actually clears her? → **B** · The paper was locked before she came, and she was never there alone
- **Q6** (Mugs) What does the background of the photo tell us? → **C** · It was taken in Room 204, and the green mug's owner is worth finding
- **Q7** (WhatsApp group) What is the most reasonable reading of this? → **D** · Someone with access took it and sent it to Aman just before 8:01
- **Q8** (Timeline) Using every time you know, when was the photo taken? → **C** · Between 7:10 PM and 8:01 PM

**Twist.** New evidence has come in: the department visitor register, the clerk's desk, and a remark about the green mug.

- **T1** (Visitor register) Which two facts break Bhatia's story? → **B** · He stayed 30 minutes for a one-minute errand, and the tiffin never left
- **T2** (Desk drawer) Which earlier clue becomes far more important now? → **A** · The post by “Aman B.” at 8:05 PM
- **T3** (The green mug) What most likely happened? → **D** · Bhatia opened the cupboard with the spare key and sent his son the photo

**A good report** names the culprit, says how it was done, and points to at least two clues that prove it (and ideally one clue that clears the first suspect).

## CASE-C · The Museum Heist

**Who did it:** Rekha Bhandari (jewellery designer)

**What happened.** Rekha Bhandari swapped the real necklace for a replica she had made in advance (stamped R.B.) on Sunday evening, between 7:20 and 7:25 PM, while the curator was out on a phone call. She held the necklace with her back to Mr. Anil, who has poor eyesight, although she said she never touched it. The boy's Saturday sketch and the cleaner's Sunday 8 AM log show the real necklace, with its lion clasp, was there until Sunday. The case was not opened at night, so the camera gap is irrelevant. Ramesh Bose shares the initials but had a green wristband and could not enter the gallery. Joseph owes money but was at the main gate from 7 to 9 PM. Rekha's studio was nearly bankrupt.

**Common wrong answer:** Joseph Pinto (night guard)

**Round 1**

- **Q1** (The necklace) The case is intact and locked, yet the necklace inside is a fake. What does that say about the thief? → **B** · They stood at the open case at some point, with a copy ready
- **Q2** (Sketchbook) A child's sketch is not expert evidence. Why is it still useful here? → **D** · Because it records a detail that only the real necklace has
- **Q3** (Cleaner's log) Put the sketch and the cleaner's note together. What do they narrow down? → **A** · The real necklace was still there at 8 AM on Sunday
- **Q4** (Alarm and CCTV) The camera was off for an hour. Why is that hour NOT when the theft happened? → **C** · Because the lock sensor shows the case stayed shut all night
- **Q5** (Key box) The real necklace was there at 8 AM on Sunday and the case stayed shut all night. When was the swap? → **D** · On Sunday evening, while the case was open for the guests
- **Q6** (Initials) Two people share these initials. What is the sensible next step? → **C** · Find out which R.B. could get near the open case on Sunday evening
- **Q7** (Guest list) What do the wristbands change? → **B** · They rule out one R.B., who could not enter the gallery
- **Q8** (The guard) Most people suspect Joseph. What is the strongest point in his favour? → **A** · At the only time the case was open, two colleagues place him at the gate

**Twist.** New evidence has come in: statements from the curator and from another guest who was in the gallery.

- **T1** (Curator's statement) While the case was open, for how long was the necklace out of the curator's sight, and who was with it? → **A** · About five minutes; Rekha and Mr. Anil
- **T2** (Guest's statement) What does Mr. Anil's statement do to the case? → **C** · It contradicts “never touched it” and gives a moment when no one saw her hands
- **T3** (Guest's statement) What do “nearly bankrupt” and “weeks to make” tell us together? → **D** · It was planned well ahead, by a skilled maker who needed money

**A good report** names the culprit, says how it was done, and points to at least two clues that prove it (and ideally one clue that clears the first suspect).

## CASE-D · The Missing Person

**Who did it:** Kavya Nair (understudy)

**What happened.** Kavya Nair, the understudy, lured Ananya to the old costume store room B-07 with a message at 9:08 PM, locked her in with a new padlock and wrote a fake goodbye note on the mirror. The note is in Kavya's blue gel pen with her spelling mistake “definately”; Ananya uses a black fountain pen and spells the word correctly. Ananya's wallet, ID, keys and charger were left behind, and the janitor heard knocking from the basement at 9:40. Kavya said she left at 8:45 and had no contact, but she messaged Ananya at 9:08 and the gate register shows 10:20. If Ananya cannot perform, the lead goes to Kavya. Dev was at the light console in view of the crew from 9:00 and kept calling her; the director left at 9:15. Ananya is found safe in B-07.

**Common wrong answer:** Dev Malhotra (stage manager, her ex)

**Round 1**

- **Q1** (Her bag) The note says she left on her own. What does the bag say? → **B** · She did not expect to be gone: what she would need is still here
- **Q2** (Mirror note) How strong is this clue on its own? → **D** · It is a hint that needs a second sign to back it up
- **Q3** (Spelling) Now add the spelling to the pen. What do the two show together? → **C** · Two separate habits of the writer are not Ananya's habits
- **Q4** (Light console) Could Dev have taken Ananya anywhere after 9:05 PM? → **B** · No: the whole crew had him in view until 10:30
- **Q5** (Thursday) Most people suspect Dev. How strong is the case against him? → **A** · A reason to look at him, but it is weak and nothing places him with her
- **Q6** (Gate register) Which two things together make the director unlikely? → **D** · He left the campus at 9:15, and he loses most if she is gone
- **Q7** (Understudy) This gives Kavya a motive, nothing more. What should be checked next? → **A** · Whether her statement about leaving at 8:45 PM is true
- **Q8** (Janitor) Put this beside the bag she left behind. What should the searchers do first? → **C** · Search the basement rooms straight away

**Twist.** New evidence has come in: Ananya's phone has been unlocked, and the stage key board and the gate register have been checked.

- **T1** (Her phone) How much of Kavya's statement is now shown to be false? → **C** · Both parts: when she left, and that she had no contact with Ananya
- **T2** (Key board) Why does the new padlock matter? → **B** · Someone locked that room very recently, and the knocking came from the basement
- **T3** (Kavya's script) Which two earlier clues does Kavya's script match? → **A** · The pen and the misspelling in the mirror note

**A good report** names the culprit, says how it was done, and points to at least two clues that prove it (and ideally one clue that clears the first suspect).
